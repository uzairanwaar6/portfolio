# Qor-Protocol

**Role:** Lead Consultant — Qordata Labs  
**Technologies:** .NET Core, React.js, SQL Server, FFmpeg, OpenAI, Azure App Service, Azure Blob Storage, PowerShell

## Overview

Contributed to the development of **Qor-Protocol**, an AI-powered audio transcription and summarization platform that transforms spoken recordings into readable transcripts, concise summaries, and actionable insights.

The solution combines **audio processing, speech transcription, and OpenAI-based summarization** to make recorded information easier to access, review, and understand.

## The Problem

Audio recordings often contain valuable discussions, important information, and key takeaways that are difficult to retrieve without listening to the entire recording.

Manually reviewing lengthy recordings and extracting meaningful information is time-consuming.

The objective was to **convert unstructured audio into accessible, meaningful information through automated transcription and AI-powered summarization**.

## Key Capabilities

- **Audio Processing:** Uses FFmpeg as part of the audio-processing workflow.
- **Speech Transcription:** Converts spoken audio into readable text.
- **AI-Powered Summarization:** Uses OpenAI to generate concise summaries of recorded content.
- **Actionable Insights:** Extracts useful information from recordings to make their content easier to review.

## Solution Architecture

The application combines a **.NET Core backend, React.js frontend, SQL Server, FFmpeg, and OpenAI integration**.
